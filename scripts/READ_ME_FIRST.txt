DUPE SWEEP

1. Drag "Dupe Sweep" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

If your Mac says it cannot verify the app (this happens with apps that have not been through Apple's paid
notarization), open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Dupe Sweep.
You only need to do this once.

Dupe Sweep never uploads anything. It moves duplicate files to the Trash, never straight to deletion, so you can
put them back.
